### Material Options:

#### 1. SLS Nylon PA12 (PA2200)
- **Properties**: High strength and durability, excellent chemical resistance
- **Best for**: Most versatile and established 3D printed material
- **Finishes**: Natural, Vibro Polished, Shot Peened, Vapour Smoothed, Colors
- **Colors Available**: Natural White, Black, Red, Blue, Green, Yellow
- **Ideal for eyewear**: Yes - most versatile option with full color range

#### 2. MJF Nylon PA12
- **Properties**: Balance between performance and reusability
- **Best for**: Manufacturing final parts and functional prototypes with fine detail
- **Finishes**: Natural, Vibro Polished, Shot Peened, Vapour Smoothed
- **Colors Available**: Grey (natural), Black (dyed only)
- **Ideal for eyewear**: Good for prototypes and complex shapes

#### 3. SAF PA11 Eco
- **Properties**: Exceptional toughness and impact resistance
- **Best for**: Demanding applications, most sustainable option
- **Finishes**: Natural, Vibro Polished, Shot Peened
- **Colors Available**: Natural, Black (dyed only)
- **Ideal for eyewear**: Good for durable frames, eco-friendly option

#### 4. SLS Flexible TPU
- **Properties**: Most flexible material, superior temperature resistance
- **Best for**: Wearable tech, custom insoles, flexible applications
- **Finishes**: Natural, Black Dyed
- **Colors Available**: White (natural), Black (dyed)
- **Ideal for eyewear**: Good for flexible components, nose pads, temple tips
